# Human list: product intent (`human:product-intent`)

Asked of the owner once per high-risk delivery, together with the evidence packet.

- The intent in one sentence, as the owner gave it: is it met?
- What the end user understands on arriving at the screen, without any explanation.
- What is missing or rings false, in product words.
- Answer: accepted, accepted with reservations, or rejected.

The orchestrator attaches what the machine has already checked (tests, captures of the journey,
reviews). The owner does not redo that work.
