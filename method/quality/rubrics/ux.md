# Rubric: user experience

1. **One journey, one goal** — one question, one answer, one button. No primary button on a
   read-only screen.
2. **States** — empty, normal, error, loading, read-only. A missing state is a finding.
3. **Words** — one label per notion; no technical or internal term shown to the end user; a state
   is never conveyed by color alone.
4. **Language** — the typography of the product's language (for instance, non-breaking spaces
   before `: ; ! ?` in French), one consistent form of address, no final period in a title or a
   button.
5. **Errors** — say what happened and what to do next; never a raw error.
6. **Confirmations** — a small confirmation is one sentence and two buttons, in a dialog, with a
   fallback when scripts are unavailable.
7. **Accessibility baseline** — keyboard, visible focus, accessible names, targets of at least
   44 px, mobile at 390 px.

Out of scope: personal taste with no effect on the journey.
