# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project, whatever their experience, background or identity, without harassment. We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep the community open, welcoming and respectful.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being kind and specific. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully, and saying so when you are wrong.
- Assuming good faith, and asking before assuming otherwise.
- Keeping discussions on the topic of the project.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unsolicited promotion.
- Anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may lock a thread or block a person for behavior they judge inappropriate. The maintainer will explain the decision when it is useful to do so.

## Scope

This applies in the project's spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub: open a private security advisory titled "Conduct report" on the repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled with discretion.

## Consequences

The maintainer decides, in proportion to the behavior: a correction, a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent block.
